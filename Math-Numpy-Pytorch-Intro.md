## Spectral theories in mathematics

- What the term "spectral theory" means.
- Why artificial neural networks fit into spectral theory.
- The difference betwen "complicated" and "complex."
- Why deep learning is easy, complicated, and complex!

There are many spectral theories in mathematics, but the idea of all the spectral theories of mathematics is that you start with something complicated and you break it up into a lot of individual simple components. So start with something complicated and you decompose it into a bunch of simple individual things.

This is a fairly reductionist perspective that you can understand something complicated by understanding all of these simple parts. It's a little bit like a rainbow. How the visible light spectrum comprises light at multiple different frequencies, and all of these different waveforms, all of these different light frequencies, sum together to give us our visible light.

So for example, there is the Fourier transform,which is used in signal processing, time series analysis, and also an image analysis.
![concepts22.png](/img/concepts22.png)

And the idea of the Fourier transform is that we can take a complicated signal or image and decompose that signal into the sum of sine waves, and each sine wave has its own frequency,
phase, and amplitude. So the sine wave is a really simple component, it's this pure sine wave,
and we say that this complicated looking signal can be decomposed and represented using the sum of very simple individual parts, which would be the sine wave.

![concepts23.png](/img/concepts23.png)

And if you studied linear algebra, then you've come across eigendecomposition or the singular value decomposition, and this follows from the spectral theory of matrices. And the idea there is that a matrix can be represented as the sum of simple rank one matrices, where each rank one matrix is defined by the outer product of some column vector and some row vector scaled by an individual number. Don't have to know this...just an example of how something complicated is broken into simple components.
